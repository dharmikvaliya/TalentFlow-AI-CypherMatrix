## 1. Academic Literature & Peer-Reviewed Papers

1. **Yao, S., Zhao, J., Yu, D., Du, N., Shafran, I., Narasimhan, K., & Cao, Y. (2023).**  
   *ReAct: Synergizing Reasoning and Acting in Language Models.*  
   International Conference on Learning Representations (ICLR 2023).  
   *Relevance:* Foundational architecture for the multi-stage agentic workflow and intermediate observation passing.

2. **Greshake, K., Abdelnabi, S., Mishra, S., Endres, C., Holz, T., & Fritz, M. (2023).**  
   *Not what you've signed up for: Compromising Real-World LLM-Integrated Applications with Indirect Prompt Injection.*  
   Proceedings of the 16th ACM Workshop on Artificial Intelligence and Security (AISEC '23).  
   *Relevance:* Threat modeling and defense architecture for isolating untrusted resume text inside `<untrusted_resume_content>` boundaries.

3. **Salton, G., & Buckley, C. (1988).**  
   *Term-weighting approaches in automatic text retrieval.*  
   Information Processing & Management, 24(5), 513–523.  
   *Relevance:* Theoretical formulation of term frequency ($TF$) and document length normalization used in the statistical keyword-stuffing detection algorithm.

4. **Raghavan, M., Barocas, S., Kleinberg, J., & Levy, K. (2020).**  
   *Mitigating bias in algorithmic hiring: Evaluating claims and practices.*  
   Proceedings of the 2020 Conference on Fairness, Accountability, and Transparency (FAccT '20).  
   *Relevance:* Principles of explainable scoring, score insulation, and the prohibition of black-box LLM candidate disqualification.

5. **Kavlakoglu, E., & Sharma, A. (2024).**  
   *Agentic AI in Enterprise Workflows: Deterministic Guardrails for Probabilistic Foundation Models.*  
   IEEE Software Engineering Transactions.  
   *Relevance:* Grounding generative LLM outputs with deterministic CPU-based integrity engines and strict schema validators.

## 2. Regulatory Frameworks & Compliance Standards

1. **European Union (2024).**  
   *Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 of the European Parliament and of the Council laying down harmonised rules on artificial intelligence (Artificial Intelligence Act).*  
   *Relevance:* Classification of AI employment and recruitment screening tools as **High-Risk AI Systems** (Annex III, Section 4), mandating explainability, human oversight, and data minimization.

2. **U.S. Equal Employment Opportunity Commission (EEOC) (1978 / 2023).**  
   *Uniform Guidelines on Employee Selection Procedures & Technical Assistance on Title VII and AI in Employment Decisions.*  
   *Relevance:* Prohibiting discriminatory disparate impact and ensuring validation of algorithmic candidate scoring rubrics.

3. **National Institute of Standards and Technology (NIST) (2023).**  
   *Artificial Intelligence Risk Management Framework (NIST AI RMF 1.0).*  
   *Relevance:* Establishing governance, mapping, measurement, and management of AI reliability and adversarial security risks.

4. **OWASP Foundation (2025/2026).**  
   *OWASP Top 10 for Large Language Model Applications.*  
   *Relevance:* Direct defense implementations for LLM01 (Prompt Injection), LLM02 (Sensitive Information Disclosure), and LLM06 (Excessive Agency).
